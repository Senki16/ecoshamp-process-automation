About

The project

EcoShamp is a university project on the design and automation of a small-scale production line for natural shampoo — integrating sustainability, efficiency and modern control systems. It was developed for CAP (Control Automático de Procesos) at Universidad EAFIT.

Sustainability

Natural ingredients, free of animal derivatives.

Automation

Every stage run by sensors, actuators and controllers.

Education

A hands-on project applying process-control theory.

Real impact

Efficient production with a smaller environmental footprint.

Project objective

Design an automated and sustainable production line for natural shampoo, integrating sensors, actuators and control systems into an efficient, safe and environmentally friendly process — with 2,000–3,000 units per day in a 12 × 2.3 × 2.3 m plant.

  • Sustainable ingredients
  • Automated and efficient process
  • High-quality final product

Impact

EcoShamp is aimed at people aged 18–45 who value sustainability and natural products, and shows how automation and engineering can contribute to responsible consumption and a cleaner future.

Mission

Produce high-quality personal-care products based on natural, sustainable ingredients, using automated processes that minimise environmental impact — effective, ethical and respectful of the environment.

Vision

Be leaders in sustainable and natural personal-care products, standing out for innovation in automated processes and commitment to the wellbeing of customers and the planet.
